# 2-minute demo script — build backward from this

**Beat 1 (0:00–0:15) — the empty plan, IN the browser.**
Open the side panel next to a blank tab. Say: "We're researching whether
there's a market for premium swimwear in Kenya." Type it in. Six objectives
appear — Market Size, Competition, Customer Demand, Pricing, Team &
Execution, Regulatory & Distribution Risk — all at "no evidence." Say: "This
isn't a chatbox. It's a standing research plan the browser feeds evidence
into."

**Beat 2 (0:15–0:45) — evidence lands where it belongs.**
Open a real article about the Kenyan swimwear/activewear market. Click
"Capture this page." Narrate the activity feed: "🔎 checking page against
open objectives… 📊 Market Size: medium confidence (1 source)." The
objective's badge changes color and a one-line summary appears — not a
generic answer, a claim mapped onto a specific checklist item.

**Beat 3 (0:45–1:20) — the rubric moment: sources disagree (memorize this).**
Open a second, contradicting article — different price range for the same
segment. Click "Capture this page" again. A contradiction banner appears
under Pricing: "⚠️ Sources disagree: A says KES 3,500–6,500, B says KES
8,000+." Say: "The agent didn't average these or pick one — it stopped and
told us." Click **Keep A** (or **Needs more research**) — the objective
updates, confidence resets to reflect the resolved disagreement.

**Beat 4 (1:20–1:40) — the agent works independently too.**
Switch to the chat sidebar. Ask it to fill in "Team & Execution" — it calls
search_web (real Exa), add_source, and update_objective live, chained in
one turn. Narrate: "Same objectives, same evidence rules, whether I'm
browsing or asking directly."

**Beat 5 (1:40–1:55) — the payoff.**
Point at the plan: "Strong on demand and market size. Weak on team and
manufacturing. That's a real first-pass diligence call, not a summary."

**Beat 6 (1:55–2:05) — the line.**
"Research Room — an agent that maintains a research plan while you browse,
and tells you when the evidence disagrees instead of guessing."

## Rules for filming

- Record the REAL app. One take, live Exa calls. A slightly slow real search
  beats a fast fake one — judges can smell canned data.
- If Exa rate-limits mid-take, don't cut: say "watch the fallback kick in"
  and let OpenRouter/the seeded state carry it. Grace under failure is a feature.
- Have the two contradicting articles pre-opened in background tabs before
  filming — don't burn demo time searching for them live.
